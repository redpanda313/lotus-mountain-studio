About

A small studio with a big alien universe

Lotus Mountain is the independent studio of Pete Jones, based in Utah. The studio designs, draws and animates its own 2D game content: characters, creatures, bosses, plants and interface art, all built around a consistent sci-fi world.

Everything is rigged and animated by hand in Spine and tested inside Unity, so the same assets used in our own game are production-ready for other developers' projects.

Featured project

Narcalid

Narcalid is our original sci-fi 2D game. Its look comes from hand-drawn characters and bone-driven Spine animation: alien creatures that move with their own personalities, boss encounters, living alien plants, a playable crew and the interface that ties it together.

The art and animation pipeline built for Narcalid is the foundation for the Spine packs we create for other Unity developers.

  • Robots & droids
  • Alien creatures
  • Bosses
  • Alien plants
  • Playable characters
  • Crew NPCs
  • Game UI
